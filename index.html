ONE REAL EXAMPLE · HURRICANE KATRINA, 2005The pump jump was much larger than the crude move alone.

WTI rose $2.13 per barrel across the cited weeks—about 5 cents per input gallon. U.S. regular pump prices rose 46 cents per gallon the following survey week. Refining and product supply disruptions mattered too; this is not a complete causal breakdown.
